Frequently asked questions

How does the subscription work?
Numae requires a monthly or yearly subscription, after a one-month free trial. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
How do I manage or cancel my subscription?
In Numae: Settings › Manage subscription. Or in the iPhone Settings app: your name › Subscriptions. The subscription is handled by Apple.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Nothing is deleted or locked. Everything stays readable; only new entries and edits are paused until you renew.
I already paid, but Numae asks me to subscribe.
On the subscription screen, tap “Restore Purchases”. Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account that subscribed.
How do I get my data on a new iPhone?
Numae keeps everything on the device. Your data is part of your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer): restore that backup on the new device.
Can I lock Numae?
Yes: Settings › Security. Numae then asks for Face ID, Touch ID or the iPhone passcode each time it opens, and hides your amounts in the app switcher.
Does Numae connect to my bank?
No. Entry is manual, and nothing leaves your iPhone.
