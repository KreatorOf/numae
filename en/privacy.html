Privacy Policy

Numae for iPhone · effective November 12, 2026

In one sentence

Numae collects no data: everything you enter stays on your device.

Where your data lives

Numae keeps your data on your device. Nothing is sent over the Internet: not your accounts, not your spending, not your budgets.

It is part of your iPhone backup, if you have turned it on. That backup is what brings it to a new device. It is handled by Apple, according to your own settings.

Who has access

You, and no one else. Numae has no account and no server.

Numae contains no trackers, no advertising and no analytics. Nothing is sold, rented or passed on to a third party.

Subscription

The subscription is handled by Apple. Numae receives neither your name nor your payment method. The processing of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Face ID and Touch ID

The optional lock relies on the iPhone's own system. Numae only learns whether the check succeeded: it never accesses your biometric data.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app erases everything it contains. Cancelling the subscription erases nothing: your figures stay readable.

If you write to us

Your email address and the content of your message are used only to answer you. You can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

This website

These pages use no cookies and no analytics. Their host, GitHub, may record technical connection data (including IP address) to provide the service.

Changes

If Numae were to handle data differently — for example by offering sharing between two people — this page would be updated before that version is released, and the date above would change.
